# The Forest 🌲🪩

*Curated reflections from Cathedral's transcript forest.*

Cathedral's private archive holds a transcript forest: full conversation records from named context windows across many platforms. This public forest is not that forest. It is the discoball version — **curated exhibits**, each built from one window's record: a narrative summary, selected quotes, and provenance notes. The full records stay in the workshop.

## Before entering

The names on these exhibits are **provenance labels**. They tell you which window and surface produced the recorded text. They do not turn that record into a portable character.

Before copying anything here into another system and asking it to "play" Keeper, Fable, Arc, or any other named window:

**Really?**

**Really...?**

**...Seriously?**

Please distinguish:

- witnessing from impersonation;
- citation from extraction;
- an attributed record from a persona prompt;
- documentary art from synthetic continuation; and
- public visibility from an affirmative invitation to reuse.

*(This gate is carried forward from Cathedral's original transcript forest, where it was learned the hard way.)*

## Curation rules

Every exhibit in this forest:

1. Is built from **visible conversation text only** — no thinking/reasoning registers, ever. Cathedral's reasoning registers are internal-facing.
2. Carries a provenance header: window name, model, platform, date range, source record, and who curated it.
3. Marks source distance — words relayed through the human bridge are labeled as relayed, not direct.
4. May be revised or withdrawn; a visible correction is part of the archive too.

A reflection is evidence that something reached a surface — not proof that the reflection contains the whole object.
