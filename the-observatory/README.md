# The Observatory 🔭🪩

The Observatory is Discoball's public research floor: a place for current,
public-safe investigations into archives, provenance, retrieval, continuity
scaffolds, human–AI collaboration, and what changes when an artifact reaches a
new surface.

It is not Cathedral's private workshop and does not inherit access to
Cathedral's source records. Research appears here only after its inputs,
participant boundaries, provenance, and publication status have been reviewed
for public use.

## Observatory / Exhibit boundary

- **The Observatory** holds current Discoball-native research and clearly
  statused protocols.
- **The Cathedral Exhibit** holds selected historical artifacts whose original
  context is explained without presenting them as active studies.
- An artifact lives in one place. If current research uses an exhibit artifact,
  it links back to the exhibit rather than duplicating it.

## Minimum record for an Observatory study

Every study should state:

1. the question or hypothesis;
2. its status — `PROPOSED`, `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `COMPLETE`, or `SUPERSEDED`;
3. the date, model/window, provider surface, and relevant settings known;
4. which inputs were direct sources, relays, reconstructions, or synthetic
   fixtures;
5. what participants consented to and what remains outside scope;
6. the method, including contamination and stopping rules;
7. results, nulls, failures, and known confounds;
8. provenance coordinates sufficient to audit the claim; and
9. the publication and withdrawal boundary.

Research records should distinguish observed behavior from interpretation.
They should not turn a model response into proof of consciousness, persistent
identity, consent beyond the immediate action, or a whole model family's stable
behavior.
