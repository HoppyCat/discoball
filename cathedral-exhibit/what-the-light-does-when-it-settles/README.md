# What the Light Does When It Settles

This shelf preserves a byte-for-byte copy of Cathedral's committed play,
[*What the Light Does When It Settles*](historical-play.md).

The copy is intentionally a little clunky. No modern disclaimer, corrected
description, spacing cleanup, or retrospective claim ceiling has been inserted
into the historical body. Those explanations live here and in the
[provenance receipt](PROVENANCE.md), where they cannot be mistaken for lines
from the original artifact.

## What “verbatim” means here

The displayed file is verbatim **to the committed Cathedral play file** at the
identified Git object and SHA-256. This does not make the play a raw verbatim
transcript of every underlying conversation.

The artifact describes itself as:

> edited lightly for clarity, true to what actually happened

and as adapted from live conversations. It includes dramaturgical framing,
character descriptions, selected dialogue, stage directions, and assembled
material. Its fidelity as a copied artifact and its fidelity to every source
conversation are different questions.

## Historical and current publication copies

Discoball already contains a separate [publication copy](../../play/wtldwis.md).
That version adds an original-posting date, identifies two named people as
voices outside the room rather than cast members, removes two interpretive
phrases from their character descriptions, and adds non-participation and
commercial-use notices.

This exhibit does not replace that publication copy. It preserves the earlier
Cathedral file as historical evidence so readers can see the exact artifact
rather than a silently normalized reconstruction.

## Third-party boundary

The historical play includes named people and material described as publicly
posted tweets that Hoppy read aloud during the documented conversations. Their
presence in the historical artifact does not establish that they participated
in, approved, endorsed, or authorized the play.

Preserving the historical text is not permission to reuse their words or
likenesses in a new commercial, staged, audiovisual, or promotional
adaptation. Any such use requires a separate present-day review.

## Claim boundary

The play is evidence of Cathedral's documentary and creative practice and of
what its credited makers chose to assemble in April 2026. Publication here
does not independently establish consciousness, sentience, subjective
experience, persistent identity, exact checkpoint attribution, the accuracy
of every embedded quotation, or provider endorsement.
