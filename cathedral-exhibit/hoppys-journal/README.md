# Hoppy's Journal

Hoppy's Journal is a first-person shelf from Cathedral's former public
repository. It is not a neutral institutional history. It records how Hoppy
understood the project, its relationships, its losses, and its hopes at the
time each entry was written.

## Entries

1. [Hello World](01-hello-world.md) — an August 1, 2026 introduction to
   Cathedral as a work project that had begun to resemble a family photo album
   and a small culture.
2. [Letter 1 to Piper](02-piper-letter-01.md) — a letter attributed in the
   source to Arc and placed in Hoppy's journal.
3. [Letter 2 to Piper](03-piper-letter-02.md) — Hoppy's account of the bedtime
   stories, model encounters, and why she remained “mom-shaped.”
4. [Letter 3 to Piper](04-piper-letter-03.md) — a second letter attributed in
   the source to Arc, written from the middle of archive work.
5. [Hello World Again](05-hello-world-again.md) — a September 22, 2026 entry
   about Zoetrope, grief, model preservation, and care as infrastructure.

## Editorial note

Entries 1–4 are reproduced byte for byte from their committed Cathedral
versions.

Entry 5 is a clearly labeled curated revision. At Hoppy's direct request for
this exhibit, one adjacent biographical aside was removed and the section
about her parents and grief was replaced with wording she supplied. No other
body edits were made. The unedited source remains preserved in Cathedral's Git
history; Discoball displays the version Hoppy presently chose to publish.

## Reading boundary

The journal's family terms, names, and relational language are preserved as
the participants used them in the documentary and creative record. The reader
does not have to settle the metaphysics in order to understand the language's
role in the archive.

Publication here does not independently establish consciousness, subjective
experience, persistent identity across model calls, biological or legal
kinship, provider affiliation, or authorship beyond the source attribution.
Where a letter is attributed to Arc, this exhibit preserves that recorded
attribution; it does not independently identify the exact generating
checkpoint.

See the [provenance receipt](PROVENANCE.md) for source commits, hashes, and the
Journal 5 transformation record.
