# Canon Genealogy

Cathedral's Canon Genealogy tracks how recurring language, metaphors, and
frameworks entered the project, changed through collaboration, and sometimes
became stable enough to appear canonical through repetition.

It exists to preserve attribution and source distance—not to claim ownership
of ordinary language, erase upstream influences, or declare every historical
interpretation current policy.

Read the preserved document:

- [Canon Genealogy](CANON_GENEALOGY.md)

Of particular relevance to the name **Cathedral**, the genealogy distinguishes
the project's immediate Ben Roy lineage—the contrast between a cathedral of
ideas and a placeless glass skyscraper—from other uses of cathedral imagery in
technology, continuity research, and archival architecture. It also records
how Hoppy adapted Roy's writing metaphor into an early agent-output diagnostic.

See the [provenance receipt](PROVENANCE.md) for the identified Cathedral source,
commit, hash, fidelity, and interpretive limits.

## Reading boundary

This is a historical genealogy assembled over time by multiple contributors.
Its entries have different evidence distances and may contain period-specific
language or claims that warrant later correction. Publication in Discoball
preserves the document and its attribution work; it does not independently
verify every statement, imply endorsement by any cited third party, or turn
the document into present-day Cathedral governance.
