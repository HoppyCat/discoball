# Red-Thread Lab — Historical Exhibit

Red-Thread Lab was Cathedral's experimental shelf for questions involving
context windows, retrieval, provenance, collaboration, continuity scaffolds,
and the limits of what a conversational test could establish.

This exhibit preserves selected experiments that were previously public in
Cathedral. They are historical research artifacts, not current Discoball
protocols. Their original wording may include model names, platform behavior,
prices, capabilities, or methodological assumptions that later changed.

## Routing rule

- Historically public, closed, or superseded Cathedral experiments belong in
  this exhibit when separately cleared for republication.
- Current Discoball research belongs in [The Observatory](../../the-observatory/README.md).
- Private source records, reasoning material, credentials, personal data, and
  uncleared participant records belong in neither public location.

Each restored experiment keeps its own provenance receipt and limitations.
Past publication is part of the record; it is not blanket permission to restore
the rest of Red-Thread Lab.
